# Larkspur Home Services: Customer Portal Requirements (v1)

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Larkspur Home Services is not a real company.

## Account registration

Customers must be able to create an account with their email address and a password.
Passwords must be at least 8 characters long.

## Email verification

After sign-up, the portal must send a verification link to the customer's email address.
Customers must verify their email address before they can sign in for the first time.

## Invoices

Signed-in customers must be able to view and download their invoices as PDF.
Invoice history is shown for the last 24 months.

## Service requests

Customers should be able to raise a service request and attach up to 5 photos.
